## 🔭 Project — Court of Claims Parser

> Transforming historical U.S. Court of Claims opinions into structured, machine-readable datasets through hybrid algorithmic and AI-assisted extraction.

#### 📖 About the Project

The **United States Court of Claims** is the federal court responsible for hearing monetary claims against the U.S. government, including contract disputes, military pay, tax refunds, patent compensation, and other financial claims. This project automates the process of converting these opinions into structured JSON datasets by combining traditional parsing techniques with modern AI methods. These methods extract information such as case numbers, decision dates, case outcomes, and case types. The project aims to build a scalable, high-accuracy extraction pipeline capable of processing decades of Court of Claims opinions into structured datasets suitable for search, analytics, and legal research.
